# Attitude Sensors

* IMU - measures acceleration and angular acceleration
* Gyroscope - measures angular velocity
* Magnetometer - measures magnetic field
* Star Tracker - deterministically identifies orientation
* Sun sensors - mostly used for solar keep out zones

# Translational Sensors

* IMU - see above
* GPS - measures position
* Ranging - from ground stations, provides ephemeris

# Actuators

* Reaction Wheels
* Magnetorquers
* Thrusters
* CMGs
