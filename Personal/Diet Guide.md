Losing weight is never fun, but this is a loose guide to make it slightly less painful and more consistent. 

1. Eat until you are just barely full. Don't overeat. This is the single most important part. Only grab what you think you need and if you're still hungry after 20-30 minutes then you can get more. 
2. No eating out of packages, decant any snacks if needed into pre-sized portions and eat only that. Every refill is a conscious decision. 
3. Make healthy snacking easier and unhealthy snacking harder. Pre-slice fruit, frozen edamame, minimize processed snacks laying around.
4. Distract yourself during most snacky hours. Between 3 and 6, make sure to keep busy. Work on hobbies, finish important work, walk the dog, go to the gym, anything to avoid snacking until dinner. 
5. Two-day rule: never take off more than one day at a time. This doesn't mean going to the gym every other day, but rather do *something* that constitutes a dedicated movement event. Gym, walk, hike, ski day, etc. 
6. Consider the "am I really hungry?" test. Don't eat if an apple or baked potato doesn't sound good.
