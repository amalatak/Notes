Writers
* Homer - Illiad and Odyssey, heroic poetry
* Hesiod - Preserved Greek myths in Theogeny and also wrote about ordinary rural life
* Aeschylus - tragedy writer
* Sophocles - Oedipus and Antigone
* Euripides
* Heroditus - wrote about Persian wars and some myths
* Thucydides - Peloponnesian war

Philosophers
* Socrates - Wrote nothing but founded Socratic method, executed in 399 B.C.
* Plato - Student of Socrates, wrote the Republic, Symposium, Phaedo, huge influence
* Aristotle - Student of Plato, teacher of Alexander the Great, wrote other famous works
