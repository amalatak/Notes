# Macroeconomics

Aggregate output is the sum of the value of all goods and services products. GDP is a common measure for this (does not include intermediary goods). Another measure would be the income model, or the value added model. Nominal GDP is the simple sum of all prices x quantity. Real GSP is adjusted for inflation and is calculated relative to a baseline year.

Inflation is calculated with the consumer price index (CPI). The GDP deflator is calculated by looking at the ratio of real GDP to nominal GSP. These measures are usually aligned. Core CPI removes volatile prices such as food and energy. 

Unemployment rate is difficult to calculate since who is participating in the labor force is not trivial to determine. Surveys are generally used. Labor force is the sum of people with a job and those looking for a job. Labor force participation rate is the labor force size relative to the able body population. 

Equilibrium output is determined in short (before goods prices adjust while asset prices are adjusting), medium (prices adjust sufficiently), and long term. Short run is business cycle time-scale, long term is growth. 

 In the short run, equilibrium output is determined by (aggregate) demand which interacts with production and income. 

Aggregate Demand is composed of 
* Consumption - (C) goods and services purchased by consumers
* Investment - (I) the sum of residential (housing, real estate) and nonresidential (companies, factories, equipment) investments
* Government spending - (G) Purchases of goods and services by federal, state, and local government, excluding government transfers (direct payments, like negative taxes)
* Exports - (X) Purchases of U.S. goods and services by foreigners
* Imports - (Im)Purchases of foreign goods and services by U.S. consumers, firms, and the U.S. Government
* Inventory investment - difference between production and sales (stuff sits around for a little while before it's sold). *Assumed to be 0 for now*

Generally, consumer spending is the biggest driver of equilibrium output which is why people pay so close attention to consumer spending sentiment. For the output of the U.S. economy, we are talking about the total demand for domestic U.S. goods and services.

Aggregate demand, Z is defined as 
$$
Z = C+I+G+X-IM
$$
